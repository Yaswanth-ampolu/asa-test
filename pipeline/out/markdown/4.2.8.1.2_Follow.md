# 4.2.8.1.2 Follow

4.2.8.1.2 Follow
For the Follow message, the time stamp is identical to the lower 14 bits of PTBclk (see 3.3.19) at the
instant, when the first symbol of the first physical layer block of this TDD burst being sent will pass
the local MDI.
If PTBclk is not locked or not running, the time stamp is undefined.
