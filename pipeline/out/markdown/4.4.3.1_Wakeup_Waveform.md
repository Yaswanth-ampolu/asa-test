# 4.4.3.1 Wakeup Waveform

4.4.3.1 Wakeup Waveform
Channel Definition
The channel of a point-2-point link consists of a cable harness and two MDIs (one at each side)
bridging the end connector of the cable harness to the transceiver.
MDI | cable harness | MDI
ASA | ASA
TX/RX | TX/RX

board components connectors board components
Figure 4-37: Parts of ASA channel definition
The propagation delay from one ASA Transceiver through MDI, cable harness and the second MDI is
limited to 82.5ns.
INFORMATIVE: Although ASA specifies a single channel for all five Speed Grades, it is understood,
that each Speed Grade will not require a controlled channel beyond a bandwidth of 70% of the baud
rate (of the respective Speed Grade).
