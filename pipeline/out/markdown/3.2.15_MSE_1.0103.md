3.2.15MSE (1.0103)
This register is optional. If the register is provided it is recommended that the calculation is
defined and controlled by the implementer. The MSE shall not be usedfor further qualification
(e.g. for Interoperability Testing). For qualification purposes the mandatory SQI information is
defined.
The MSE value for ASA PHY should be determined with a refresh rate in the tolerance range of 82µs–
220µs.The resulting MSE value shall be linearly scaled toa value in the range of [0…511].
