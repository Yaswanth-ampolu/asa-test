# 3.2.8 ASAnodeIRQ (1.0008)

3.2.8 ASAnodeIRQ (1.0008)
This register applies to the entire ASA node and serves as initial information, in which domain or sub-
block a deviation from nominal operation has occurred.
ASA defined error conditions in the different parts shall also lead to the issuing of a flag here.
Additional implementation specific conditions may be tied into the flags in this register.
