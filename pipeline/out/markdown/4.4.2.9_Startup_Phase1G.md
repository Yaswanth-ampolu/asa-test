# 4.4.2.9 Startup Phase1G

4.4.2.9 Startup Phase1G
The transmitter during startup Phase1G shall use the nominal voltage of Speed Grade 1. See section
4.4.2.2.1. Other Speed Grade 1 specifications do not apply.
