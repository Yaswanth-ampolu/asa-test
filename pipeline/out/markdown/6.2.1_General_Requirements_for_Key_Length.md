# 6.2.1 General Requirements for Key Length

6.2.1 General Requirements for Key Length
All devices shall support DKs, BKs, and LKs as 128bit AES keys.
All devices may support DKs, BKs, and LKs as 256bit AES keys.
If a device supports a 256bit AES BK, it shall support a 256bit AES DK as well.
If a device supports 256bit AES LKs, it shall support a 256bit AES BK and 256bit AES DK.
Each Device shall keep track of the Lengths of all installed Keys.
