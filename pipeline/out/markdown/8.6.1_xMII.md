# 8.6.1 xMII

8.6.1 xMII
MLE supports data rates, which fall into the speed range of MII, GMII and XGMII.
The data received on XGMII, GMII or MII shall begrouped for 64bit block format according to “IEEE
802.3 Standard for Ethernet, Clause 49”.
The following table shows the mapping of MII and GMII signals to XGMII signals for the sole purpose
of creating a valid 64bit block format used in ASA MLE.

TXD<7:0>, TXD<7:0>, | TXD<3:0>, TXD<3:0>, TXD<3:0>, TXD<3:0>,
t3 | t2 | t7 | t6 | t5 | t4
TXD<31:0>
TXD<7:0>, TXD<7:0> | TXD<3:0>, TXD<3:0>, TXD<3:0>, TXD<3:0>
t1 | t0 | t3 | t2 | t1 | t0
RXD<7:0>, RXD<7:0>, | RXD<3:0>, RXD<3:0>, RXD<3:0>, RXD<3:0>,
t3 | t2 | t7 | t6 | t5 | t4
