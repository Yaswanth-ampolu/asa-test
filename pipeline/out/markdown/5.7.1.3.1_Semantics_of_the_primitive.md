# 5.7.1.3.1 Semantics of the primitive

5.7.1.3.1 Semantics of the primitive
DLP_RX.oamUnit(size, header, dll_payload, phylStat, dllStat)
The DLL uses DLP_RX.oamUnit to provide the header and the payload of a received container, along
with status information, to the DLP_RX of the OAM.
For parameters, see 5.7.1.2
