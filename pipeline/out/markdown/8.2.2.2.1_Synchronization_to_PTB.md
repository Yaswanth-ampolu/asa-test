# 8.2.2.2.1 Synchronization to PTB

8.2.2.2.1 Synchronization to PTB
In an ASA SerDes Branch topology beyond point-to-point, it is important to keep TDD cycles aligned
after the StartTDD command (see 5.5.3.9). The PCS in the root node (i.e. clock leader) and any link-
local clock leader therefore starts a new TDD cycle on a fixed value period of PTB tics (with a
tolerance of ±1).
