# 4.2.2.5.3 Upstream Speed Grade 1 and 2

4.2.2.5.3 Upstream Speed Grade 1 and 2
The physical layer block tx_phy_block<1727:0> is scrambled to tx_phy_block_scr<1727:0> with the
additive scrambler for Upstream as defined in section 4.2.5.
The scrambler output Sis applied as an additive scrambler sequence to tx_phy_block, whereas the
scrambler is advanced by one shift for every one bit in tx_phy_block and the first output bit of the
scrambler is applied to the MSB of tx_phy_block, such that
tx_phy_block_scr<MSB> = tx_phy_block<MSB>⨁ 𝑆
