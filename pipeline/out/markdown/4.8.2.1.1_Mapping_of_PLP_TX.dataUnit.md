# 4.8.2.1.1 Mapping of PLP_TX.dataUnit

4.8.2.1.1 Mapping of PLP_TX.dataUnit
The Primitive PLP_TX.dataUnit from PLP_TX<dll> shall be demultiplexed round robin over PLP_TX<n>
ascending starting with Lane 0 (after the transition out of Startup, see section 2.4) to the maximum
Lane index 1-3 implemented.
