# 5.6.1.2 DLP_TX.dataUnit

5.6.1.2 DLP_TX.dataUnit
5.6.1.2.1 Semantics of the primitive
DLP_TX.dataUnit(ase_payload)
The ASE uses DLP_TX.dataUnit to provide the payload of a container to the DLL. The size shall be the
size, which has been requested.
5.6.1.2.2 When generated
After reception of DLP_TX.indicateSlot (see 5.6.1.1), if payload is available.
5.6.1.2.3 Effect of receipt
DLL uses the provided ase_payload in the next container to be sent.
