# 4.4.1.1.2 Linearity Test

4.4.1.1.2 Linearity Test
This test mode is used for testing the transmitter linearity. Using the test fixture shown at Figure
4-28. A known PRBS13Q test pattern is transmitted and measured at output of MDI at DUT.
The pattern shall be recorded 16 times for the linear fit of the pulse response and oversampled with
an integer factor M of 7 or larger. The pulse response shall be matched with a length of 5 baud rate
periods.

The transmitter SNDR, shall exceed 38dB in SG5 and SG4, and 32dB in SG3, and 30dB in SG2, and
28dB for SG1. (Note: The values are chosen to limit SNR loss less than 1dB due to TX Nonlinearity.)
For SG4 and 5, a test pattern PRBS13Q is used to do the linearity test for PAM4 signaling, and the
PRBS13Q pattern is defined in 4.4.1.1.2.1. For SG1, SG2 and SG3, a test pattern of PRBS9 is used to
do the linearity test for PAM2 signaling, and the PRBS9 pattern is defined at 4.2.2.3.4.
The full pattern is repeated 128 times, when triggered via startup.
