# 4.2.7.1.1 Phase1G semantics of m_ptb

4.2.7.1.1 Phase1G semantics of m_ptb
The m_ptb<15:0> for the resynchronization header for startup Phase1G contains the value of
ph1G_retry_count (see 4.3.3.1) indicating the number of repeats/detect-tries of the Phase1G
sequence.
For the handling of ph1G_linkID and ph1G_groupID, see section 4.8.4.1 in the case of Link
Aggregation.
The field ph1G_linkID also contains the seed of the scrambler when no Link Aggregation is used (see
section 4.2.5).
Bit(s) Name | Description
15:12 ph1G_retry_count<3:0> Lower 4 bits only
11:10 ph1G_groupID<1:0> | Identification of a group of MDIs
9:8 ph1G_linkID<1:0> | Identification of a particular MDI/Link within a group
7:0 ph1G_retry_count<7:0> Full 8 bits of counter

INFORMATIVE: the field is padded with lower nibble of the counter to avoid stationary patterns in
repeats of Phase1G.
