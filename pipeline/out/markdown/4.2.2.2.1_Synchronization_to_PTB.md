# 4.2.2.2.1 Synchronization to PTB

4.2.2.2.1 Synchronization to PTB
In an ASA SerDes Branch topology beyond point-to-point, it is important to keep TDD cycles aligned
after the StartTDD command (see 5.5.3.9). The PCS in the root node (i.e. clock leader) or node, which
is link-local clock leader, therefore starts a new TDD cycle every 6844 PTB tics (with a tolerance of
±1).
