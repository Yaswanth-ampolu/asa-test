# 4.2.2.4.1 Downstream Speed Grade 1 and 2

4.2.2.4.1 Downstream Speed Grade 1 and 2
A physical layer block contains 642 bytes payload and 3 times 2 bytes FEC parity from encoder
4.2.4.2, where the first byte from d_plp_tx<7:0> is m of the first FEC message, the 215thbyte from
d_plp_tx<7:0> is m of the second FEC message and the 429thbyte from d_plp_tx<7:0> is m of
the third FEC message. Each byte from d_plp_tx is mapped as <7:0> onto Reed Solomon symbol bits
bto b.
d_plp_tx<7:0> | d_plp_tx<7:0>
(t=0) | (t=641)
... 0 7 | 0 7 | ... 0 7 | 0 7 | ... 0 7 | 0 7
m | m m p p m | m m p p m | m m p p
first FEC codeword | second FEC codeword | third FEC codeword
