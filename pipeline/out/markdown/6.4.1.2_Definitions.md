# 6.4.1.2 Definitions

6.4.1.2 Definitions
The following terms are used for describing the primitives:
•
Implicit Data: parameters that are authenticated but not transmitted and not encrypted.
They are usually determined by other header fields (e.g. header fields in the OAM).
•
Additional Data: parameters that are authenticated and transmitted but not encrypted.
•
Data (encrypted): parameters that are authenticated, encrypted, and transmitted.
•
Enc.: Encrypted (yes, no, n/a)–n/a meaning “not applicable”.
•
Auth.: Authenticated (yes, no, n/a)–n/a meaning “not applicable”.
