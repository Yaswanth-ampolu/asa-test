# 4.8.2.2.1 Mapping of PLP_RX.dataUnit

4.8.2.2.1 Mapping of PLP_RX.dataUnit
The Primitive PLP_RX.dataUnit is multiplexed onto the PLP_RX<dll>.
The Link Aggregation Sublayer shall learn the differential delay between the Lanes during Startup and
compensate it after Startup to preserve the order of DLL containers from the transmitting side.
