# 3.2.16 FECstat (1.0104)

3.2.16FECstat (1.0104)
NOTICE: This register is expected to be non-zero in the vast majority of installations. The closer the
used channel is to the maximum electrical channel specification, the faster this register will
saturate. This is normal operation and is not an indication of a device fault.
