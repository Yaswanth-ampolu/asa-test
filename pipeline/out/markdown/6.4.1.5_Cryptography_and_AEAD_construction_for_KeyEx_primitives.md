# 6.4.1.5 Cryptography and AEAD construction for KeyEx primitives

6.4.1.5 Cryptography and AEAD construction for KeyEx primitives
The messages shall be protected and encrypted by AES-GCM.
The device shall support AES-GCM with AES128.
The device may additionally support AES-GCM with AES256.
