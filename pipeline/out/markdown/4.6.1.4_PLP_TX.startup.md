# 4.6.1.4 PLP_TX.startup

4.6.1.4 PLP_TX.startup
4.6.1.4.1 Semantics of the primitive
PLP_TX.startup(PTBstamp)
DLL/OAM uses this primitive to trigger the execution of the startup sequence.
PTBstamp can have two values: either 0 at the initial startup (when PTB is in reset state) or the future
value of PTBclk, when the first symbol of Phase1G shall pass the MDI (root side) or wait state for
Phase1G sequence is reached (near-side node).
