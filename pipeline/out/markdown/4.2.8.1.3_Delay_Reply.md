# 4.2.8.1.3 Delay Reply

4.2.8.1.3 Delay Reply
For the Delay Reply message, the time stamp is identical to the lower 14 bits of PTBclk (see 3.3.19) at
the instant, when the first symbol of the first physical layer block of the last TDD burst received
passed the local MDI.
