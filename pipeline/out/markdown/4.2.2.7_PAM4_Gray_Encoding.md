# 4.2.2.7 PAM4 Gray Encoding

4.2.2.7 PAM4 Gray Encoding
The PCS transmit function shall map consecutive pairs of bits of tx_phy_block_scr to consecutive
Gray-coded PAM4 symbols starting with the MSB of tx_phy_block_scr.
Each pair of bits, {MSB, LSB} of tx_phy_block_scr is mapped to one Gray-coded PAM4 symbol M(j) as
follows:
{0, 0} maps to 1,
{0, 1} maps to 1/3,
{1, 1} maps to -1/3,
{1, 0} maps to -1.
