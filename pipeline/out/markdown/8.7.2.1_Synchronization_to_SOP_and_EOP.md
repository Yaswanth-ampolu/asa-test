# 8.7.2.1 Synchronization to SOP and EOP

8.7.2.1 Synchronization to SOP and EOP
The receiver shall scan for SOP character to detect the start of a frame.
The receiver marks the time the when the group of two control characters JK of the SOP passed the
receiving MDI. If the OAM message will be used to initialize the PTB, the delta between the received
time and the time the message was processed will be added to the timestamp prior to initializing the
PTB.
