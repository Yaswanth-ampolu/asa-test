# 4.2.8.3.3 Delay Reply

4.2.8.3.3 Delay Reply
On the reception of a Delay Reply message, the Clock Follower uses the time stamp it took for its
own DelayRequest:
t_PTB_tx … PTBclk, when the first symbol of the first physical layer block of the last TDD burst
being sent passed the local MDI
and the TDDstamp in Table 4-8as “DREPLYstamp”, and the time stamps taken with the last Follow
message (see 4.2.8.3.2) and calculates
𝑃𝑇𝐵𝑜𝑓𝑓𝑠𝑒𝑡 = | (𝑡_𝑃𝑇𝐵_𝑟𝑥 − 𝐹𝑂𝐿𝐿𝑂𝑊𝑠𝑡𝑎𝑚𝑝 − 𝐷𝑅𝐸𝑃𝐿𝑌𝑠𝑡𝑎𝑚𝑝 + 𝑡_𝑃𝑇𝐵_𝑡𝑥)/2
Equation 4-15: PTBoffset calculation
The clock follower shall use this calculated PTBoffset to update its local PTBclk.
If the absolute value of PTBoffset is within ptb_LOCK_tol (see 4.2.8.4.1), the PTBclk update is deemed
to be in sync. This information is kept internally on PTBint_syncedUpdates.
The PTBdelay can be calculated as
𝑃𝑇𝐵𝑑𝑒𝑙𝑎𝑦 = | 𝑡_𝑃𝑇𝐵_𝑟𝑥 − 𝐹𝑂𝐿𝐿𝑂𝑊𝑠𝑡𝑎𝑚𝑝 − 𝑃𝑇𝐵𝑜𝑓𝑓𝑠𝑒𝑡
or
𝑃𝑇𝐵𝑑𝑒𝑙𝑎𝑦 = | 𝐷𝑅𝐸𝑃𝐿𝑌𝑠𝑡𝑎𝑚𝑝 − 𝑡_𝑃𝑇𝐵_𝑡𝑥 + 𝑃𝑇𝐵𝑜𝑓𝑓𝑠𝑒𝑡
Equation 4-16: PTBdelay calculation
The clock follower shall use this calculated PTBdelay to update the register in 3.3.20.
