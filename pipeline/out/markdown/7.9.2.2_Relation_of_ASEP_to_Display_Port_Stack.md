7.9.2.2 Relation of ASEP to Display Port Stack

Boundary, after (optional) HDCP encoder and before the skew insertion / scrambler (see
[asep_dp_01], Figure 2-8). The following diagram illustrates, how the ASA Motion Link is working as a
substitute for DP physical layer.
