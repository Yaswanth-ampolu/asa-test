# 4.2.2.5.2 Downstream Speed Grade 4 and 5

4.2.2.5.2 Downstream Speed Grade 4 and 5
The physical layer block tx_phy_block<5759:0> is scrambled to tx_phy_block_scr<5759:0> with an
additive scrambler for Downstream as defined in section 4.2.5.
The scrambler outputs Sand Sare applied as an additive scrambler sequence to tx_phy_block,
whereby the scrambler is advanced by one shift for every two bits in tx_phy_block and the first tuple
of output bit of the scrambler is applied to the first two MSB of tx_phy_block, such that
tx_phy_block_scr<MSB> = tx_phy_block<MSB>⨁ 𝑆
tx_phy_block_scr<MSB-1> = tx_phy_block<MSB-1>⨁ 𝑆
and every following tuple {S,S} is applied to every following tx_phy_block<n:n-1>.
