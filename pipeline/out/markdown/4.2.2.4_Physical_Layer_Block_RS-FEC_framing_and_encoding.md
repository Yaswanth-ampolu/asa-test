# 4.2.2.4 Physical Layer Block: RS-FEC framing and encoding

4.2.2.4 Physical Layer Block: RS-FEC framing and encoding
The physical layer blocks are transmitted with MSB first; i.e. tx_phy_block<5183> for 4.2.2.4.1,
tx_phy_block<5759> for 4.2.2.4.2 and tx_phy_block<1727> for 4.2.2.4.3.
