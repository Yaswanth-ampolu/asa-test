# 4.2.3.1 Burst synchronization

4.2.3.1 Burst synchronization
Using the symbols provided by the PMA Receive, the PCS shall detect and decode the
Resynchronization Header (see 4.2.2.3) and use it to detect and correct signal polarity as well as align
to the physical layer blocks.
