# 6.4.2 Mapping of Primitives to OAM messages (KeyEx)

6.4.2 Mapping of Primitives to OAM messages (KeyEx)
All fields in OAM messages shall be in Big Endian (BE).
Reserved Bytes shall be set to 0x00 on sending and ignored on receiving.
TheIDfield refers to the KeyExchange Primitive ID.
TheParametersfield refers to the KeyExchange Primitive Parameters without additional stuffing
bytes.
Each KeyEx OAM Message shall be transported in an exclusive Container and shall not be grouped
with other OAM Messages, therefore CADnext field (see 5.5.3) shall always be 0.
Informational: The Status Code of a KeyExchange Response is included in theParametersfield (Byte
0).
