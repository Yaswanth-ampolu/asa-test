# 7.7.1.2 SPI ASEP Data Conversion Period

7.7.1.2 SPI ASEP Data Conversion Period
The“SPI ASEPdata conversion period” (SPI_DCP) is defined as the duration of SPI bus activity (on
master side) converted into one SPI ASEP packet.
SPI_DCP has to be equal or larger than SPI_STC.
