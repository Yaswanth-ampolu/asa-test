# 4.5.1 Cable Harness Insertion Loss

4.5.1 Cable Harness Insertion Loss
The given insertion loss is the valid limits for Speed Grades 1 through 4. For Speed Grade 5, the cable
insertion loss limits in Equation 4-29 and Equation 4-30 shall be valid with a factor 0.8 over the entire
frequency range.
The cable harness insertion loss limit shall be met including the effect of inline connectors.
