In this test mode, the receiver immunity against specified crosstalk and disturbers is verified. After
startup, the receiver is operating as in Normal Mode on PCS/PMA at the relative speed grade and
when fed by a signal according to 4.4.1.1.6, the BER after FEC decoding is expected to be less than
-12when received over a channel according to 4.5. The PMA receive functions operates according
to 4.3.2.3 and 4.3.2.4.
At the beginning of this test mode, registers in 3.2.13 and 3.2.16 are reset, incremented during test
mode as applicable, and are held at the end of this test mode to be read out.
When triggered via startup, the internal bit error test is run for 500 ms, after which the external
pattern has to be shut off within 20 ms and link partner listens on the MDI to receive the result.
When triggered via startup, the result of the test is sent back with a Startup Phase1G (see 4.2.7.1),
where the info field inf is being replaced by the contents shown in Table 4-23. Before sending the
1G
