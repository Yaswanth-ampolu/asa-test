# 4.2.3.2 PCS descrambler

4.2.3.2 PCS descrambler
The PCS shall use the synchronization of 4.2.3.1 to start/stop the side stream scrambler to and
reverse the transmit side scrambling by applying the same operation as described in 4.2.2.5.
