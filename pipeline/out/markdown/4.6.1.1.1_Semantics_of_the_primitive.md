# 4.6.1.1.1 Semantics of the primitive

4.6.1.1.1 Semantics of the primitive
PLP_TX.dataUnit(d_plp_tx<7:0><0:len-1>)
The DLL uses PLP_TX.dataUnit to provide the array of bytes of a DLL container, where
d_plp_tx<7:0>,<0> is the first byte of the container (i.e. first byte of the header).
The parameter “len”can have two values:
for Downstream
for Upstream
