# 4.4.2.4.1 Transmitter peak output

4.4.2.4.1 Transmitter peak output
With MDI for STP terminated by 100Ω, the differential nominal amplitude at the MDI shall be 1.0V
peak-to-peak.
With MDI for Coax terminated by 50Ω, the nominal amplitude at the MDI shall be 0.5V peak-to-
peak.
