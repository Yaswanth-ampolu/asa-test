# 6.2.2 Device Keys

6.2.2 Device Keys
Each ASA Device shall have one symmetric Device Key DK.
A Device Key (DK):
•
should be unique over all devices of an OEM.
•
forms the root of trust between the OEM and the device.
•
and thereby can be used to prevent counterfeit devices.
The device key itself is calculated from two sub-keys:
•
The Device Key Tier-1 part (DK_0) is persistently written to the ASA Device by the Tier1
during ECU production.
•
The Device Key OEM part (DK_1) is persistently written to the ASA Device by the OEM during
car production.
Device Key shall be calculated as a XOR operation of DK_0 and DK_1.
DK_0 and DK_1 shall each have the same size as DK.
