# 6.4.1.1 Locations of Key Exchange Entities

6.4.1.1 Locations of Key Exchange Entities
For an ASA Root Device, the Key Exchange Entity shall be located at the ASA Root ECU Software.
For an ASA Root Device, there shall be one Key Exchange Entity per ASA Branch.
For a non-Root ASA Device, the Key Exchange Entity shall be located at the ASA Device itself.
