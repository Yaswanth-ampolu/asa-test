# 8.1.2 Organization of Section 8

8.1.2 Organization of Section 8
The sections 8.2 through 8.5 (MLE) mirror the sections 4.2 through 4.5 (ML).
If there is no change given in the respective subsection 8.{section subnumbering}, the corresponding
subsection 4.{section subnumbering} is referenced directly after the section headlinewith “See
equivalent subsection in section 4”and applies to the MLE Physical Layer without any alterations,
except with section references changes from 4.x.y.z to 8.x.y.z (where x.y.z represent the section
subnumbering of any hierarchy depth).
If this equivalancy is given for a section 8.x, the entire section 4.x is valid including its entire sub-
hierachy of chapters 4.x.y.z (where y.z represent the section subnumbering of any hierarchy depth).
If any changes are required, the subsections are replaced as a whole. A new subsection with same
subnumbering is created in section 8. For these altered sections, the entire corresponding subsection
in section 4 does not apply.
