# 5.3.2 Header Decoding & Demultiplexing

5.3.2 Header Decoding & Demultiplexing
For this section “targetIDx” refers to any of targetID0, targetID1, targetID2 or targetID3.All other
fields are referred to by their names defined in 5.2.2.
For header decoding, both cases 5.3.2.1 and 5.3.2.2 need to be checked for all targetID in the header.
Special cases 5.3.2.3 and 5.3.2.4 cover the OAM stream during enumeration.
If the header cannot be decoder or does not match any of the cases, 2.2210.15:8 (see 3.3.25) shall be
incremented and the header with payload shall be discarded.
