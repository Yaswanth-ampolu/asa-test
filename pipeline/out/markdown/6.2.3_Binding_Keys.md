# 6.2.3 Binding Keys

6.2.3 Binding Keys
A Binding Key (BK) is binding an ECU to a vehicle to prevent theft parts from functioning in another
vehicle.
Each pair of Root Device (representing the vehicle) and a Non-Root Device connected to the Root
Device shall have one shared Binding Key (BK).
Binding Keys shall be symmetric.
Bindings Keys shall be changeable.
Binding Keys shall support a minimum of 16 write cycles.
A device might break, when installing a binding key more often than designed for.
Binding Keys shall not be readable from outside the ASA nodes on the device (including OAM
Channel).
Binding Keys shall be persistent to a power cycle.
