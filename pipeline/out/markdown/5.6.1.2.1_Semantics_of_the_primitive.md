# 5.6.1.2.1 Semantics of the primitive

5.6.1.2.1 Semantics of the primitive
DLP_TX.dataUnit(ase_payload)
The ASE uses DLP_TX.dataUnit to provide the payload of a container to the DLL. The size shall be the
size, which has been requested.
