# 4.2.2.5.1 Downstream Speed Grade 1, 2 and 3

4.2.2.5.1 Downstream Speed Grade 1, 2 and 3
The physical layer block tx_phy_block<5183:0> (SG 1 and 2) or tx_phy_block<5759:0> (SG 3) is
scrambled to tx_phy_block_scr<5183:0> or tx_phy_block_scr<5759:0> respectively with an additive
scrambler for Downstream as defined in section 4.2.5.
The scrambler output Sis applied as an additive scrambler sequence to tx_phy_block, whereby the
scrambler is advanced by one shift for every one bit in tx_phy_block and the first output bit of the
scrambler is applied to the MSB of tx_phy_block, such that
tx_phy_block_scr<MSB> = tx_phy_block<MSB>⨁ 𝑆
