# 7.4.2 Usage of Format

7.4.2 Usage of Format
INFORMATIVE: this ASEP stream type is unidirectional. The vast majority of Video Data ASEP Packet
types to be transmitted are Video Line packets. It is recommended, that the Pixelclk Packet is sent
between two groups of Video Line Packets each representing a frame. It is at the discretion of the
implementer, how often a Pixelclk packet is required to keep the information up to date. An
application may also not require the information of the pixelclk packet.
ASEP Format: I2C
There are two I2C ASEP modes: Byte Mode and Bulk Mode, see section 7.5.4 for more background
information.

same format:
