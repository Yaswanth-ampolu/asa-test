# 5.7.1.1 DLP_RX.dataUnit

5.7.1.1 DLP_RX.dataUnit
5.7.1.1.1 Semantics of the primitive
DLP_RX.dataUnit(size, dll_payload, phyLStat, dllStat)
The DLL uses DLP_RX.dataUnit to provide the payload of a received container, along with status
information, to an ASD or OAM.
phyLStat is equal to errStat in 4.7.1.1.
dllStat … is the DLL status
decode_good | no error
duplicate_packetID | packetID in header is smaller than fullPacketID[4:0]+1 (see 3.5.1.1)
missing_packetID | packetID in header is larger than fullPacketID[4:0]+1 (see 3.5.1.1)
5.7.1.1.2 When generated
When the Receive Process of the DLL decodes a header targetID, which is associated to a DLP_RX
with another stream type than “Forward Data”.
5.7.1.1.3 Effect of receipt
See section 7.2.
