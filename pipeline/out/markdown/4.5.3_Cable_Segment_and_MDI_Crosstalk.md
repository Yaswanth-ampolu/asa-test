# 4.5.3 Cable Segment and MDI Crosstalk

4.5.3 Cable Segment and MDI Crosstalk
The cross talk limits shall be met for the combination of near-end MDI, the cable harness and the far-
end MDI.
Crosstalk is defined as two components: Power Sum Alien Near End Crosstalk (PSANEXT) and Power
Sum Attenuator to Alien Crosstalk Ratio Far End (PSAACRF).
