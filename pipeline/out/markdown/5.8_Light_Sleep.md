# 5.8 Light Sleep

Light Sleep
The support of Light Sleep feature is optional.
Light Sleep enables duty cycling of the traffic of the link on a time scale of a sensor or display cycle, it
can stop traffic on the link for up to 4096 TDD cycles (or nominally 112,132,096ns) and has a
minimum lower boundary for the time to go to Light Sleep. That minimum boundary is mediated by
the root node for every Light Sleep event.
INFORMATIVE: The minimum sleep time lies in the order of 250µs.
The Light Sleep state
- Stops Mapper, extends the quiet gap
- PMA TX and RX are disabled
All other functions of Normal Mode remain on, especially PTBclk is required to keep running on a
local reference.
During Light Sleep, the PTBclk is allowed to drift a maximum of 140 PTB tics from its nominal
position.
INFORMATIVE: 140 PTB tics is equal to 560 ns, which is deemed a tolerable search window for a
Phase1G startup pattern length of 3.28µs with a resynchronization header of 384 ns.
