# 5.7.1.2 DLP_RX.forwardUnit

5.7.1.2 DLP_RX.forwardUnit
5.7.1.2.1 Semantics of the primitive
DLP_RX.forwardUnit(size, header, dll_payload, phyLStat, dllStat)
The DLL uses DLP_RX.forwardUnit to provide the header and the payload of a received container,
along with status information, to the DLP_RX of FoFa.
Header … unmodified DLL header
For other parameters, see 5.7.1.1
