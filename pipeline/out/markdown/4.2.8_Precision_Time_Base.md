# 4.2.8 Precision Time Base

4.2.8 Precision Time Base
Every ASA node has a logical clock PTBclk (see 3.3.19) with a resolution of 4 ns (called a PTB tic). An
ASA node is either a (local) clock leader or a clock follower. There is only one SerDes Branch global
clock leader, which is always the root node.
Coarse synchronization of the follower’s PTBclk is achieved through an info field in the OAM header
from the root node (see 5.5).
Fine synchronization of the follower’s PTBclk is achieved throughcommunication between (local)
clock leader and clock follower on a single point to point connection, where the coding of
Follow/DelayRequest/DelayReply into the resync header is described in 4.2.2.3.5.
The semantics and calculations for both message types are described in the remainder of this section
0.
The clock leader controls the sequence of follow and delay reply messages, Delay Request is always
sent by the follower and does not require an immediate Delay Reply. The clock leader shall at least
send one Follow message between two Delay Reply messages. The clock leader shall send a
minimum of one Delay Reply message for every 15 follow messages.
INFORMATIVE: a ratio of 7 Follow messages to 1 Delay Reply message is recommended.
Local clock leaders copy the PTBclk of the clock follower in the same ASA device once the clock
follower is locked. The definition of the lock state reflected in 3.3.20 is described in 4.2.8.4 in detail.
The deviation of PTBclk in the clock leader(s) and the clock follower in the same device shall never
exceed one PTB tic when clock follower is in locked state.
The clock follower shall achieve a PTBlock condition within 100 TDD cycles after the “PTB initial copy”
(see section 4.2.8.3.1.1) and the recommended ratio of 7 Follow messages to 1 Delay Reply message.
