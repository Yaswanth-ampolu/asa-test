# 8.2.2.5.2 MLE Modes based on Speed Grade 4 and 5

8.2.2.5.2 MLE Modes based on Speed Grade 4 and 5
The scrambler outputs Sand Sare applied as an additive scrambler sequence to tx_phy_blockE,
whereby the scrambler is advanced by one shift for every two bits in tx_phy_blockE and the first
tuple of output bit of the scrambler is applied to the first two MSB of tx_phy_blockE, such that
tx_phy_blockE_scr<MSB> = tx_phy_blockE<MSB>⨁ 𝑆
tx_phy_blockE_scr<MSB-1> = tx_phy_blockE<MSB-1>⨁ 𝑆
and every following tuple {S,S} is applied to every following tx_phy_blockE<n:n-1>.
