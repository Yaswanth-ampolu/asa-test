# 1.3.4 ASA Motion Link

1.3.4 ASA Motion Link
ASA Motion Link is the brand name for all ASA technologies/devices. Devices based on chapters 2-7
of the ASA Transceiver Specification arereferred to as “ASA Motion Link” or “ASA Motion Link
SerDes”. Device based on chapter 8 are referred to as “ASA Motion Link Ethernet” (MLE).
