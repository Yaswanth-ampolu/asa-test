# 4.3.2.3 PMA Receive Function

4.3.2.3 PMA Receive Function
The PMA receive function contains circuitry to decode a PAM2 or PAM4 mapping (depending on
Speed Grade) and present the received sequences to the PCS. The quality of these sequences shall be
better than a BER of 10-12after RS-FEC decoding when received from a transmitter according to 4.4.2
over a channel according to 4.5.
INFORMATIVE: The PMA receive function is assumed to operate under thermal noise floor of a
maximum of -160dBm/Hz.
