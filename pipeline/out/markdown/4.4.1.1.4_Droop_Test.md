# 4.4.1.1.4 Droop Test

4.4.1.1.4 Droop Test
With the transmitter in droop test and using the transmitter test fixture 1 shown in Figure 4-28.
When in droop test, the PHY shall transmit a continuous pattern of 160 × S {+1} symbols followed by
160 × S {–1} symbols with the transmitted symbols timed from its local clock source. See Table 4-22
for the definition of S. The magnitude of both the positive and negative droop shall be less than 32%
measured with respect to an initial value at 4 ns after the zero crossing and a final value at 16 ns
after the zero crossing (12 ns period).
The pattern is run for 10 ms, when triggered via startup.
