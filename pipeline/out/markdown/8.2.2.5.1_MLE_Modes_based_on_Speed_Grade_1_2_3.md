# 8.2.2.5.1 MLE Modes based on Speed Grade 1/2/3

8.2.2.5.1 MLE Modes based on Speed Grade 1/2/3
The scrambler output Sis applied as an additive scrambler sequence to tx_phy_blockE, whereby the
scrambler is advanced by one shift for every one bit in tx_phy_blockE and the first output bit of the
scrambler is applied to the MSB of tx_phy_blockE, such that
tx_phy_blockE_scr<MSB> = tx_phy_blockE<MSB>⨁ 𝑆
