4.2.2.4.2 Downstream Speed Grade 3, 4 and 5
A physical layer block contains 642 bytes payload and 3 times 26 bytes FEC parity from encoder
4.2.4.3, where the first byte from d_plp_tx<7:0> is m of the first FEC message, the 215thbyte from
d_plp_tx<7:0> is m of the second FEC message and the 429thbyte from d_plp_tx<7:0> is m of
the third FEC message. Each byte from d_plp_tx is mapped as <7:0> onto Reed Solomon symbol bits
bto b.
