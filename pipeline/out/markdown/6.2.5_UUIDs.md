# 6.2.5 UUIDs

6.2.5 UUIDs
Each device shall support a one-time writable 128-bit number, called UUID.
If the UUID was not written yet, it shall be 0.
The UUID shall be freely readable.
The structure and mechanisms to make UUIDs unique in an OEMs area of concern, is up to the OEM.
