# 4.6.1.2 PLP_TX.nextPhyBlock

4.6.1.2 PLP_TX.nextPhyBlock
4.6.1.2.1 Semantics of the primitive
The PCS uses PLP_TX.nextPhyBlock() to request payload for another physical layer block.
4.6.1.2.2 When generated
Generated on the timing of the Physical Layer whenever a transmission burst is active.
4.6.1.2.3 Effect of receipt
DLL executes the DLL Transmit Process and responds with PLP_TX.dataUnit
