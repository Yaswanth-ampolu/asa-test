# 5.6.1.4 DLP_TX.yield

5.6.1.4 DLP_TX.yield
5.6.1.4.1 Semantics of the primitive
DLP_TX.yield()
The ASE or FoFa uses DLP_TX.yield to signal, that it has no data to send and forfeits the transmit
opportunity.
5.6.1.4.2 When generated
After reception of DLP_TX.indicateSlot (see 5.6.1.1), if no payload is available.
5.6.1.4.3 Effect of receipt
No container is constructed for this ASE. Instead, OAM is polled with 5.6.1.1.
