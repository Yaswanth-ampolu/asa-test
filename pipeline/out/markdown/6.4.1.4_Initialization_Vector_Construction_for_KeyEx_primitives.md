# 6.4.1.4 Initialization Vector Construction for KeyEx primitives

6.4.1.4 Initialization Vector Construction for KeyEx primitives
The 96bit IV for the protection of KeyEx primitives shall be constructed as follows:
•
[95:95] KeyEx = 1
•
[94:94] Direction (see section 6.4.1.7)
•
[93:64] Salt–as installed by the KeyEx or 0 if not.
•
[63:32] Power Cycle Counter as defined by the Root Device
•
[31:00] KeyEx AEAD Invocation Counter
