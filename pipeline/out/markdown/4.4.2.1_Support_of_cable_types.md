# 4.4.2.1 Support of cable types

4.4.2.1 Support of cable types
The transmitter in every speed grade supports an STP cable with a nominal 100Ω impedance and a
coax cable with a nominal impedance of 50Ω.
If not explicitly denoted, the electrical specifications in the section refer to STP cable. The
corresponding values for coax cables obtained by adjusting the value to 50% of the amplitude in Volt
or -3dB for dBm respectively.
