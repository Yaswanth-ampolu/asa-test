# 4.2.7.1 Startup Phase1G

4.2.7.1 Startup Phase1G
A startup phase1G block is a concatenation of a single SG1 resynchronization header as defined in
4.2.2.3 (with one field exchanged, see 4.2.7.1.1) and training symbols tx_start1G<1151:0> consisting
of a sequence from the scrambler polynomial, an info field and redundancy. The first bit being sent of
the resynchronization header is its LSB. The first bit being sent of the training symbols is its LSB.
The training symbols Sare obtained by shifting the LFSR of the side stream scrambler polynomial
described in 4.2.5 one time for each bit. The info field inf is defined in 4.2.7.1.2.
